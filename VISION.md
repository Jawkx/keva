# keva — Vision

**Storage that you and your agent can use.**

keva is the permanent, structured home for your files — simple enough to use today, built on a primitive that's ready for a world run by AI agents.

---

## The bet

The file system we all still use was designed for a human sitting at a desk, clicking through folders, remembering where they put things. The folder tree is a primitive from the 1970s, and it has barely changed since. It assumes the only intelligence in the loop is the person doing the filing.

That assumption is breaking. Software can now read, watch, and understand the contents of a file. But the file itself is still a blob in a tree — the system knows its name, its size, and where it sits in a hierarchy, and almost nothing else. It doesn't know what's *in* the file, what it's *for*, or how it relates to everything around it. We've taught machines to understand our files, then handed them a storage model that hides everything worth understanding.

keva starts from a different primitive. A file is not a blob to be buried in a folder. It is an **item with open-ended, structured meaning** — a set of key-value properties that anything can read, write, query, and act on. Some of that meaning is automatic (type, size, source). Most of it is yours to define. And increasingly, some of it is written by the agents working on your behalf.

Storage stops being a place you put things and forgets about them. It becomes a substrate that knows what it holds.

## What keva is

For the person who just wants their scattered files in one trustworthy place, keva is dead simple: drop files in, they're safe, they're yours, they're easy to find. No new habits to learn, no walls to climb. It's a better version of the storage you already use.

Underneath that simplicity is the part that matters. Every file in keva carries metadata — structured key-value properties, fully customizable. And every one of those properties is reachable through a clean, first-class API. You can read and write it. Your apps can. And an AI agent, connected over the API or through MCP, can query your files by meaning, enrich them with what it learns, and organize them on your behalf.

So you can ask your agent to go find what's inside all your diving videos, tag them by location and who's in them, and pull up the three best clips — and it can actually *do* that, because the files aren't opaque. They're items it can reason about.

## Who it's for

Two people, one product.

The first wants their digital life out of a dozen scattered places and into one home they trust. They never think about metadata or APIs. They just notice that things are findable, and that asking for what they want works.

The second is building the future — a developer, a tinkerer, an agent. They need a storage surface that is programmable to the core, where structure is a feature and not a workaround. keva gives them a clean primitive to build on instead of fighting a folder tree.

keva is built so the same system serves both, and so the first person grows naturally into the second as agents become a normal part of how everyone works.

## What we believe

**Files are items, not blobs.** Meaning should live with the file, in structure the system understands — not trapped inside the bytes or scattered across folder names.

**Your schema is yours.** Beyond a few sensible defaults, the metadata is open-ended. keva doesn't decide how your world is organized. You do — or your agent does, with your permission.

**Agent-native, not agent-retrofitted.** The API and agent access are not a feature bolted onto a consumer app. They are how keva works. The graphical app and the agent are two clients of the same programmable core.

**One source of truth.** keva is the place everything else plugs into — the canonical home your other tools, apps, and agents connect through. Not another silo to sync, but the center other things orbit.

**Simple at the surface, deep underneath.** Power should never be the price of ease. A first-time user and a fleet of agents touch the same system and each gets exactly what they need from it.

**Your data is permanent and it is yours.** keva means *fixed, settled, kept*. Files you trust to it stay yours — portable, exportable, never held hostage. Trust is the whole product. We don't get a second chance at it.

## The world we're building toward

A world where "talk to your files" is just how storage works.

Where you stop hunting through folders and start describing what you want. Where the boring work of sorting, tagging, finding, and connecting your files is done quietly by agents you've given the keys to. Where every app and model you use reaches into one trusted source instead of fragmenting your data across a hundred services. Where keva is the last storage you set up, because everything else plugs into it.

The folder tree taught us to think like filing cabinets. keva lets your files think for themselves.

## What keva is not

- **Not another folder tree.** Hierarchy is one view, not the foundation. The foundation is structured meaning.
- **Not a walled garden.** Open API, exportable data, an ecosystem of plugins. keva connects to your world; it doesn't trap it.
- **Not an AI gimmick.** The intelligence comes from a better primitive, not a chatbot stapled to a file browser.
- **Not a creative suite.** keva is where your files live and how they're understood — not where you edit them. It makes everything else better at its job.
